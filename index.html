I'm Enoch LASTNAME, an incoming Computer Engineering student at the University of California, Riverside. My long-term goal is to build affordable, high-impact technology for underserved communities. I'm especially drawn to physical products, the kind of technology people can hold and use, and I want to build them independently rather than work my way up inside existing hierarchies. That's why the technical co-founder path appeals to me: it combines engineering skill with the ownership of making something from scratch.

My path into engineering started with entrepreneurship. I started Pearl Archive, a streetwear brand, and took it from concept to real product. Running it taught me how to turn an idea into something people will buy, how to work with limited resources, and how to take responsibility when things don't go as planned. I paused the brand about two years ago, but the lessons about branding, sourcing and building an audience still shape how I approach every project.

I've also worked in food and beverage retail, where I learned to stay calm and reliable in a fast-paced environment and to treat every customer with respect. Outside of work, I've done community outreach in Tijuana, which reinforced why I want to build technology in the first place: the people who need good tools the most are often the ones least served by them.

On the technical side, I'm building my foundation now. I've worked in Python, I'm learning C++ in my first-year coursework, and I use Git and GitHub to manage my projects. My first major project is a pickup basketball app. It shows local courts on a map, and each court grows as more people log play time there. It uses geofence check-ins to detect real play, and it levels up players based on hours played, with peer-given boosts for things like being a friendly teammate or an efficient scorer. I'm building a simple first version and expanding it through trial and error, because I'd rather ship something small that works than plan something huge that never exists.

Creativity is a big part of how I work. I produce music in FL Studio, I'm passionate about streetwear and design, and I train consistently in the gym. I played football in high school and still play basketball regularly. Team sports taught me discipline, communication and how to perform under pressure, and I bring those habits into my work.

I'm currently looking for work-study and part-time opportunities where I can contribute, learn from experienced people, and grow as an engineer. If you're working on something interesting, or you just want to talk about building useful technology, I'd love to connect.
